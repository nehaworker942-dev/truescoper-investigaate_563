Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Truescope Investigation (“we”, “our”, or “us”) is committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of our clients. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you contact us or engage our services:

  • Name, phone number, email address and other contact details
  • Details related to your enquiry or case
  • Any documents or information you voluntarily share with us

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information solely for:

  • Responding to your enquiries and providing investigation services
  • Communicating with you regarding your case
  • Maintaining internal records for professional and legal purposes

3. Confidentiality

All client information and case details are treated as strictly confidential. We do not share, sell, or disclose your personal information to third parties except:

  • When required by law or legal process
  • With your explicit consent
  • To our internal team members who need the information to perform the investigation

4. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

5. WhatsApp & Communication

When you contact us via WhatsApp, the conversation remains confidential. We do not share chat history with any external party.

6. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
